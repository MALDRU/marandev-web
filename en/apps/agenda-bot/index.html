The problem

Scheduling by chat and phone does not scale

Clinics, salons, workshops and consultants lose hours answering messages, matching schedules and chasing no-shows. Agenda Bot automates that whole cycle: the customer picks a slot, the business confirms it and everyone gets a reminder.

  • No overbookingTwo appointments for the same professional never overlap: availability is checked at the very moment the appointment is saved.
  • No friction for customersFive-step booking from the phone, with no account. Customers can look up or cancel their appointment with a code.
  • Each business, its own spaceMulti-tenant: every business has its own public link, logo, schedule and team, with its data kept separate.
Features

Everything an appointment-based business needs

  • Online booking, no sign-up

    Mobile-first public page, installable as an app (PWA), with each business's name and logo. Customers choose the service, professional, day and time.

  • Telegram bot

    Each business connects its own bot (automatically or with a BotFather token) so customers can book, check and cancel from the chat.

  • Notices and reminders

    Notices to the customer when an appointment is confirmed, rejected or cancelled, and an automatic reminder a few hours before the appointment.

  • Schedules, closures and holidays

    Weekly hours per business and per professional, one-off closures and blocks, and per-country holidays calculated automatically. A preview shows what the customer will see.

  • Services, resources and customers

    Service catalog with durations, professionals or resources with daily limits and buffer time, manual or automatic confirmation, and customer records with history.

  • Plans and validity

    Plan catalog with per-business validity, monthly renewals with a payment history and expiry warnings for whoever runs the platform.

1. Who we are and what this policy covers

Agenda Bot is an appointment scheduling platform operated by Marandev (Maldru), a software developer based in Colombia. This policy explains how personal data is handled in the admin panel, on each business's public booking page and in the Telegram bot. It follows Colombian Law 1581 of 2012, Decree 1377 of 2013 (compiled in Decree 1074 of 2015) and other Colombian personal data protection rules.

2. Roles: controller and processor

Agenda Bot is used by businesses (the "companies") to serve their own customers. Therefore:

  • End-customer data (people who book an appointment): the controller is the business that collects it. Marandev acts as data processor: it processes the data on the business's behalf, only to provide the service and following its instructions.
  • Business account and panel user data (administrators): Marandev is the controller.

If you are a business's customer and want to exercise your rights, you can contact the business first; you can also write to us and we will handle your request.

3. Data we process

  • About the company: legal name, tax ID (NIT), phone, public link, logo, hours, services, resources or professionals, contracted plan, validity and recorded subscription payments.
  • About panel users: name, username and password (stored in protected form), role and session records.
  • About end customers: name, phone, notes that they or the business add, and the data of each appointment (service, professional, date and time, status and change history).
  • If they book through Telegram: the Telegram chat identifier needed to reply and send notices, and the username or name Telegram shares with the bot.
  • Technical data: IP address and server access logs, used for security, abuse prevention and troubleshooting.

We do not ask for sensitive data. We recommend that you and your business avoid writing health or other sensitive data in appointment notes.

4. What we use the data for

  • Managing the agenda: creating, confirming, rescheduling and cancelling appointments, and preventing schedule clashes.
  • Sending appointment notices and reminders through the enabled channels (currently Telegram).
  • Managing accounts, plans and each company's validity.
  • Maintaining security, preventing fraud and abuse, and fixing faults.
  • Complying with legal obligations.

We do not sell personal data or use it for advertising, and we do not share it with third parties other than the providers named in this policy.

5. Telegram and other third parties

When a business turns on the integration, messages with its bot go through Telegram, which processes data under its own privacy policy. Marandev does not control that processing. Bot tokens are stored encrypted. The platform runs on server infrastructure contracted by Marandev; no other providers receive end-customer data.

6. Cookies and browser storage

Agenda Bot uses no advertising or tracking cookies. It uses browser storage only for what is strictly necessary: keeping the panel session, remembering the light or dark theme, and enabling installation as an app (PWA) with its file cache. The marandev.co site stores only your theme preference.

7. Retention and deletion

We keep data while the company account exists, so it retains its appointment history. If a company is deleted, its data is permanently erased, including customers, appointments and history. A business can also delete a customer and all their appointments from the panel. Technical server logs are kept only as long as needed for security and troubleshooting.

When a company's plan expires, access and bookings are suspended, but data is not deleted for that reason.

8. Security

We apply reasonable technical and organizational measures: encrypted connections (HTTPS), secrets encrypted at rest, role-based access control with isolation between companies, expiring sessions, rate limits, automatic abuse blocking and controlled deployments. No system is infallible; if we detect an incident affecting your data, we will act and inform you as required by law.

9. Your rights

As a data subject you may know, update and correct your data, request proof of the authorization, be informed about how it is used, revoke the authorization or request deletion where applicable, file complaints with the Superintendencia de Industria y Comercio (SIC) and access your data free of charge. We answer queries and claims within the legal deadlines (10 and 15 business days respectively).

To exercise them, write to hola@marandev.co with your name, your phone number and the business you booked with.

10. Minors

Agenda Bot is not aimed at people under 18 as panel users. If a business books appointments for minors, the business must hold the authorization of their legal guardians.

11. Changes and contact

We may update this policy; the current version and its date will always be on this page, and we will notify companies of relevant changes. Contact: hola@marandev.co.
